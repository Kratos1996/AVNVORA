# TAJIKA_V1 knowledge pack

Status: **VERIFIED_SCOPED_CONTENT**. This pack contains source-linked adaptations of selected material from the 1907 Tājika Nīlakaṇṭhī edition. Its checksum is `513be82525a8a3b846a86b6aaf770ef1575b61fcafe196e0f41de0bd4213301d`.

Content scope: selected Muntha house indications (houses 3–6), their affliction/support qualifications, and selected Sun-as-Varshesha indications. It does not implement or calculate a complete Varshaphal chart.

The scan is recorded as public domain in India and the United States per Wikimedia Commons; territorial status may differ. The selected Wikisource transcription pages are CC BY-SA 4.0; attribution and ShareAlike are preserved. Wikisource marks those transcriptions unproofread; each was cross-checked against the corresponding scan image. See `source/source_pages.json` and [the acquisition report](../../docs/KNOWLEDGE_ACQUISITION_REPORT.md).

Rebuild the JSON pack with `python3 knowledge/tajika/build_pack.py`. Runtime API: `TajikaKnowledgePack.v1()`. Validation checks source references, rights markers, uniqueness, and chunk hashes.
