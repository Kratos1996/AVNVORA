# AYNVORA Intelligence & AI
Version: 1.0

## Boundary
Deterministic engines calculate facts. AI explains, summarizes, asks/normalizes user questions and helps present information.

## Offline Direction
A small on-device model may be used where practical. Core astrology must not depend on an LLM.

## Grounding
AI should receive structured engine results and rule metadata rather than inventing calculations.

## Safety
Do not fabricate verses, calculations, sources or certainty. Distinguish traditional interpretation from factual calculation.

## Reality Check
Where appropriate, present practical evidence/action alongside astrological perspective.
