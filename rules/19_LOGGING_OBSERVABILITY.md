# AYNVORA Logging & Observability
Version: 1.0

## Principles
Useful, minimal, privacy-aware and configurable.

## Never Log
Passwords, access tokens, private keys, secrets, full personal profiles, raw credentials or sensitive payloads.

## Levels
Debug, Info, Warning, Error, Security/Audit.

## Production
Debug logging should be disabled or tightly controlled in production builds.

## Audit
Administrative/security-sensitive events require structured audit records with appropriate retention.
