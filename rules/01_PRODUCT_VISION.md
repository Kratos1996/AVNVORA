# AYNVORA Product Vision
Version: 1.0

## Vision
AYNVORA is a professional offline-first astrology technology platform and SDK designed to turn traditional astrology calculations and knowledge into clear, understandable guidance.

## Philosophy
Ancient wisdom should support clearer choices, not fear or dependency.

The product must:
- Separate calculation from interpretation.
- Explain the basis of important results.
- Encourage practical action.
- Avoid guaranteed outcomes and sensational claims.
- Keep the user as the final decision-maker.

## Platform
- Kotlin Multiplatform
- Compose Multiplatform
- Android
- iOS
- Desktop
- B2B SDK
- Reference consumer application
- Admin/Data platform

## Core principle
Deterministic engines calculate. Intelligence layers explain and assist. UI presents approved information using the AYNVORA Design System.
