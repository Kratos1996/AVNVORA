## 1. Traditional Provenance & Source Governance

All traditional interpretations provided in AYNVORA are strictly sourced from classical Indian
palmistry literature:

- **Primary Source**: Classical *Hastasamudrika Shastra* (हस्तसामुद्रिक शास्त्र), *Bhavishya Purana*
  traditions, and *Skanda Purana* anatomical references.
- **Rule Against Fabrication**: If an approved classical textual meaning is not available for a
  given condition or line configuration, the system must return `CONTENT_UNAVAILABLE`. Under no
  circumstances may AI models or developers invent pseudoclassical references or quotes.

## 2. Supported Lines & Classical Symbolism

### 1. Life Line (*Pitri Rekha* / *Jeevan Rekha* - मातृ/पितृ रेखा)

- **Classical Conception**: Vital energy (*Prana*), constitutional stamina, and rootedness in
  physical vitality.
- **Symbolic Interpretation**: A clear, sweeping curve around the Thenar eminence (Venus mount)
  denotes grounded physical vigor, resilience, and an enthusiastic relationship with life.
- **Ethical Limitation**: Never interpreted as an indicator of lifespan, age of death, or inevitable
  illness.

### 2. Head Line (*Matri Rekha* / *Dhi Rekha* - मातृ/धी रेखा)

- **Classical Conception**: Intellectual disposition, concentration, cognitive style, and clarity of
  thought (*Buddhi*).
- **Symbolic Interpretation**: A balanced transverse line extending smoothly across the central palm
  indicates analytical balance, mental equilibrium, and deliberate decision-making.

### 3. Heart Line (*Ayur Rekha* in classical Hastasamudrika / *Hridaya Rekha* - हृदय रेखा)

- **Classical Conception**: Emotional resilience, interpersonal empathy, devotion, and affective
  expression.
- **Symbolic Interpretation**: An unbroken curve ending near the mount of Jupiter signifies elevated
  empathy, idealistic devotion, and balanced interpersonal warmth.

### 4. Fate Line (*Karma Rekha* / *Urdhva Rekha* - कर्म/ऊर्ध्व रेखा)

- **Classical Conception**: Focus, sense of life purpose, vocational application, and
  self-discipline.
- **Symbolic Interpretation**: A distinct vertical line rising toward the Saturn mount indicates
  focused vocational alignment and conscious dedication to one's life duties.

### 5. Palm Geometry (*Karatalakriti* - करतलाकृति)

- **Elemental Archetypes**:
    - `SQUARE`: Earth archetype — practical, grounded, methodical.
    - `RECTANGULAR`: Fire/Air archetype — dynamic, intellectual, expansive.
    - `LONG`: Water archetype — sensitive, reflective, intuitive.
    - `WIDE`: Earth/Fire composite — energetic, tactile, spontaneous.
