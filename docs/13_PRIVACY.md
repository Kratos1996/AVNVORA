# AYNVORA Privacy Specification
Version: 1.0

## Principles
- collect only what is needed
- purpose limitation
- data minimization
- user transparency
- retention limits
- secure deletion where applicable
- consent where required
- export/access mechanisms where required

## Sensitive Data
Birth date/time/place and family relationships may be highly personal. Treat them as sensitive product data even where legal classification differs by jurisdiction.

## Telemetry
Diagnostics must avoid raw personal/astrological data unless explicitly necessary, consented and protected.

## Third Parties
Document every external service receiving user data and the purpose of transfer.
