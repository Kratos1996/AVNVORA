## 1. Executive Summary & Quality Gate Principles

AYNVORA strictly distinguishes:

- **Numerical Disciplines**: Numerology (birth date & life cycles), Gematria (Hebrew letter-number
  equivalence), Abjad Arithmetic (Arabic Hisab al-Jummal), Magic Squares (Lo Shu topological
  patterns), and Number Symbolism (cultural/phonetic superstitions).
- **Source Quality**: Primary historical texts and recognized scholarly editions supersede internet
  calculators and blogs.
- **Ruleset Independence**: Incompatible methodologies are never silently merged or averaged. Every
  system has an independent ruleset ID, version, and golden reference tests.
- **Script Authenticity**: Script-bound systems strictly enforce native scripts (Hebrew for
  Gematria, Arabic for Abjad) and reject unsupported scripts without silent corruption.

## 3. Disciplinary Taxonomy

AYNVORA enforces distinct disciplinary modeling:

1. **NUMEROLOGY**: Calculates personal life cycles, radical traits, destiny paths, and name
   resonances from birth dates and phonetic names (e.g. Chaldean, Pythagorean, Indian Ank Jyotish,
   Agrippan).
2. **MAGIC_SQUARE**: Evaluates spatial coordinate configurations, elemental balances, and arrow
   vectors on a 3×3 matrix from calendar digits (e.g. Classical Lo Shu).
3. **GEMATRIA**: Computes numerical values of sacred or textual strings in the Hebrew alphabet (e.g.
   Mispar Hechrachi / Ragil, Mispar Katan). Not a birth-date life path system.
4. **ABJAD_ARITHMETIC**: Computes alphanumerical values according to the historical Semitic
   consonant order (*Hisab al-Jummal*). Codified by Ibn Khaldun in *The Muqaddimah*.
5. **NUMBER_SYMBOLISM**: Subjective cultural associations or repetitive number sequences without
   mathematical biographical reduction (e.g. Angel numbers, Chinese homophony). Never disguised as
   calculation engines.
