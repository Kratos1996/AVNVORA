## 2. Cross-Edition Comparative Verification (Dutt 1908 & Gita Press 1416)

- **Chapter 16 vs 17 Structural Reconcilation**:
    - **Wood 1911**: Chapter 16 contains 120 verses and seamlessly incorporates the Phala-shruti (vv
      115–120) and Suta's concluding dialogue.
    - **Gita Press Code 1416**: Splices the conclusion into an artificial 17th chapter ("सत्रहवाँ
      अध्याय").
    - **Dutt 1908**: Classifies the Pretakalpa within the Dharmakhanda, cross-confirming the
      thematic progression.
    - **Pipeline Policy**: We strictly preserve the Wood 1911 authentic 16-chapter structure. No
      synthetic Chapter 17 was created.
