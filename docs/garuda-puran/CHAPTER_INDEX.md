## 3. Discrepancy & Inconsistency Documentation

1. **Chapter Count in Saroddhara Editions**:
    - **Gita Press Code 1416 (Source 2):** Claims 16 chapters in introduction, but enumerates 17 in
      contents and body by splitting Phala-shruti into a standalone chapter.
    - **Wood & Subrahmanyam 1911 (Source 5):** Exactly 16 chapters; Phala-shruti is integrated into
      Chapter 16 (vv. 115–120).
    - **SanskritDocuments (Source 4):** Exactly 16 chapters, mirroring Wood 1911.
    - **Resolution:** AYNVORA maintains the authentic 16-chapter structure of Wood 1911 as primary.
      Phala-shruti is classified as the concluding section of Chapter 16 (`GP_WOOD_CH16_V115_120`),
      and no artificial Chapter 17 is created.

2. **Scope Difference Between Saroddhara and Classical Mahapurana**:
    - The Saroddhara focuses exclusively on the funeral liturgy, post-death journey, and soul's
      destiny (approx. 1,200–1,400 verses).
    - The classical Mahapurana (Source 3, 6, 7) contains more than 19,000 verses across 229 chapters
      of the Purvakhanda.
    - **Secondary Cross-Reference:** Dutt 1908 is maintained in `GarudaSourceComparisonRegistry` to
      document variant readings without merging texts.
